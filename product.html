Sartorial Excellence Engineered for Decades

The Cashmere Tailored Overcoat embodies our commitment to timeless luxury. Crafted from unblended 100% double-faced Mongolian cashmere sourced from ethical pastures, this piece offers superlative thermal insulation while remaining delightfully lightweight and fluid.

Each coat requires more than sixteen hours of hand-guided construction in our northern Italian partner atelier. The interior seams are bound in pure silk twill, while the horn buttons are sustainably carved and individually anchored with reinforced cross-stitching.

Key Design Highlights

  • Double-faced cashmere that eliminates bulky interior linings for a clean, natural drape
  • Slightly dropped shoulders allowing effortless layering over chunky knitwear or formal suits
  • Twin welt flap pockets with reinforced bar-tacking and concealed interior chest pocket
  • Deep center back vent allowing unrestricted stride and elegant movement
