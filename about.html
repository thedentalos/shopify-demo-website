Heritage & Vision

Born from a Passion for Mindful Tailoring

Founded in 2020 in the vibrant heart of SoHo, New York, LUXE STORE was conceived as an antidote to disposable fast fashion. We believe garments should be treasured heirlooms—crafted deliberately, finished meticulously, and designed to transcend ephemeral trend cycles.

Our journey began with a singular focus: sourcing the world's most luxurious natural fibers. From organic Italian merino wool and French flax linen to Grade-A Mongolian cashmere, each raw material is selected with unyielding respect for quality, texture, and planetary wellbeing.

Every piece in our atelier is cut and sewn in partnership with generational master artisans who bring decades of specialized heritage to life. By keeping our production limited and focused, we honor the craft of slow fashion while offering a wardrobe that exudes effortless poise.

"True luxury is not about excess; it is the quiet harmony of exceptional craftsmanship, ethical intent, and enduring beauty."
Sophia Laurent Founder & Creative Director
Guiding Principles

The Pillars of Our Atelier

Our commitments define how we design, manufacture, and curate each collection.

Quality Craftsmanship

We collaborate exclusively with master tailors and artisan ateliers. Bound seams, hand-stitched collars, and reinforced buttonholes ensure each garment stands the test of decades.

Sustainable Materials

100% of our textiles are certified renewable, recycled, or regenerative natural fibers. We forbid hazardous chemical finishes, preferring non-toxic vegetable dyes and organic washes.

Ethical Production

Fair living wages, secure working environments, and dignified livelihoods are non-negotiable standards across our partner workshops in New York, Milan, and Porto.
