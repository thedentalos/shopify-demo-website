Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

At LUXE STORE ("we", "our", or "us"), we hold your privacy in the highest regard. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, utilize, safeguard, and disclose personal data when you interact with our digital boutique, visit our Manhattan atelier, or make a purchase.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us as well as data gathered automatically when you navigate our platform:

  • Personal Identification: Full name, billing and delivery addresses, email address, telephone number, and contact preferences.
  • Transaction & Financial Information: Payment card tokenized details (handled exclusively through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processors), purchase histories, saved wishlist selections, and return records.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type and operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, time spent per session, and language preferences.
  • Bespoke Atelier Data: Tailoring measurements, fabric preferences, and stylist appointment notes provided during personalized consultations.

2. How We Utilize Your Information

Your data enables us to provide a high-touch, tailored luxury shopping experience:

  • To process and fulfill transactions, coordinate courier shipments, and administer returns.
  • To communicate transactional confirmations, shipment tracking updates, and concierge service notifications.
  • To personalize recommendations and curated editorial collections based on your styling preferences.
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraudulent transactions or unauthorized access.
  • To comply with statutory legal, accounting, and tax compliance obligations.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, and local storage mechanisms to enhance navigation performance:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for platform operations, shopping bag persistence, and secure session management.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Enable us to understand client traffic flows and diagnose site performance issues.
  • Personalization Cookies: Remember your selected currency, sizing preferences, and language settings.
  • Marketing Cookies: Help deliver non-intrusive, relevant editorial advertisements on partner platforms. You can configure your browser to decline marketing cookies at any time without impacting essential boutique checkout features.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers. We share data solely with trusted service partners under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Payment Gateways: Stripe, Apple Pay, PayPal, and Klarna for encrypted payment handling.
  • Logistics & Courier Partners: DHL Express, FedEx, and UPS for delivery fulfillment.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Enterprise cloud hosting platforms guaranteeing SOC-2 security protocols.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your geographic residency, you possess statutory rights regarding your personal records:

  • Right to Access: You may request a complete portable copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct or update inaccurate personal details via your account dashboard or by contacting concierge.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the permanent deletion of your customer profile, subject to statutory tax and recordkeeping laws.
  • Right to Opt-Out: California residents (CCPA) and European residents (GDPR) have the explicit right to opt out of marketing communications or non-essential profiling at any time.

6. Data Security & Retention

We utilize industry-standard 256-bit TLS encryption across all digital interfaces. Access to personal client information is strictly restricted to authorized concierge personnel who require access to fulfill specific client requests. We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services and satisfy legal obligations.
