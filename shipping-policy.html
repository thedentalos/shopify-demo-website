Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

At LUXE STORE, each garment is packed by hand in our New York atelier using FSC-certified archival boxes and protective canvas dust covers. We are committed to providing reliable, carbon-neutral shipping options domestically and across the globe.

1. Domestic Shipping Rates & Delivery Timeframes

We provide three convenient domestic shipping methods for destinations across the contiguous United States, Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. Territories. Delivery timelines begin once an order has been prepared and dispatched from our atelier.

Shipping Service Estimated Transit Time Order Value Under $150 Order Value $150 & Above

Express 2-Day 2 Business Days $18.00 Flat Rate $18.00 Flat Rate
Priority Atelier (Overnight) Next Business Day (by 3:00 PM) $35.00 Flat Rate $35.00 Flat Rate

2. Order Processing & Atelier Handling

Each piece is individually inspected for garment excellence prior to packaging:

  • Standard Orders: Processed and dispatched within 1 to 2 business days (Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays).
  • Express & Priority Orders: Orders placed before 1:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST) will be dispatched on the same business day.
  • Custom Tailoring & Monogramming: Please allow an additional 3 to 5 business days for personalized embroidery and custom hem modifications.

3. International Customs, Taxes & Duties

LUXE STORE ships internationally to over 45 countries using DHL Express Worldwide. All international orders are dispatched under Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) terms wherever permitted by local regulations:

  • All relevant import duties, value-added taxes (VAT), and customs clearance fees are calculated automatically and paid directly at checkout.
  • You will never be contacted by customs or freight carriers to pay additional unforeseen fees before parcel release.
  • International standard delivery typically takes between 4 to 8 business days depending on recipient jurisdiction and customs clearance speed.

4. Real-time Tracking & Delivery Notifications

Upon fulfillment, a confirmation dispatch email containing your unique tracking number and an encrypted carrier tracking link will be sent to your registered email address. You may also monitor real-time shipment updates directly through your LUXE STORE Account Dashboard.

5. Lost, Delayed, or Damaged Parcels

Every parcel leaving our facility is insured for its full retail valuation during transit:

  • Damaged Shipments: If your delivery arrives in damaged condition, please take photographs of both the outer packaging and inner garments, and notify us at concierge@luxestore.com within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Lost or Stolen Shipments: If carrier tracking indicates "Delivered" but your order cannot be located, please inspect perimeter areas and contact us within 5 calendar days. We will immediately initiate a priority carrier trace and dispatch an expedited replacement or issue a complete refund.

6. Signature Requirements & Special Handling

To protect valuable luxury purchases, orders exceeding $500 in total value will automatically require a direct adult signature upon delivery. If you prefer to waive signature requirements, you may indicate so via courier delivery management tools, though LUXE STORE cannot assume liability for unattended drop-offs.
