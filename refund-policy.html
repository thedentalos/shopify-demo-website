Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

We take immense pride in the meticulous craftsmanship of every LUXE STORE creation. If for any reason your purchase does not fulfill your expectations, we gladly offer returns and complimentary exchanges within 30 calendar days of delivery.

1. 30-Day Return Eligibility Guidelines

To qualify for a full reimbursement, returned items must comply with the following standards:

  • Condition: Unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and completely free of perfumes, deodorants, or makeup marks.
  • Tags & Security Ribbons: All original brand tags, designer labels, fabric swatch envelopes, and tamper-evident security loops must remain firmly attached.
  • Original Packaging: Garments must be returned in their protective garment sleeves, dust bags, and presentation boxes.
  • Timeframe: The return package must be postmarked or lodged with the carrier within 30 calendar days from the delivery confirmation date.

2. Step-by-Step Return Process

Returning an item is quick and seamless through our automated client portal:

1
Access Return Portal: Visit your Account Dashboard or navigate to our Return Portal and enter your Order Number and postal code.
2
Select Items & Reason: Indicate the items you wish to return and choose whether you prefer an exchange (complimentary), store credit (+10% bonus), or original payment refund.
3
Print Prepaid Shipping Label: Instantly print your prepaid domestic return shipping label and packing slip.
4
Pack & Hand Off: Place items in their original protective box, affix the shipping label over previous barcodes, and drop off at any authorized courier location.

3. Inspection & Refund Timelines

Once your parcel arrives at our Manhattan atelier, our quality assurance artisans inspect the returned merchandise within 2 to 3 business days:

  • Approval: Upon approval, your refund is processed immediately to your original method of payment.
  • Financial Institution Posting: Depending on your banking institution or card issuer, funds typically reflect in your statement within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Restocking Deduction: For domestic refunds returning to the original payment method, a $10 insured transit and handling fee is deducted. Returns for LUXE store credit or size exchanges incur no deduction.

4. Non-Returnable Merchandise

For hygiene, customization, and archival preservation reasons, certain categories are marked final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged:

  • Customized, tailored, or monogrammed bespoke garments.
  • Intimates, swimwear, and bodysuits whose protective hygiene seal has been removed or broken.
  • Pierced earrings and fine jewelry items.
  • Fragrances, candles, and skincare products once cellophane seals are broken.
  • Archive Sale or clearance items explicitly designated as "Final Sale" on product pages.
  • Digital gift cards and promotional vouchers.

5. Size & Style Exchanges

Need a different size or colorway? We provide complimentary shipping both ways on all domestic exchanges. When you select an exchange in our return portal, we reserve your replacement item immediately so your preferred size doesn't sell out before your return reaches our atelier.

6. Damaged or Defective Items

If an item arrives damaged or displays a manufacturing defect, please contact our concierge team at returns@luxestore.com within 48 hours of receipt with your order number and clear photos. We will provide an immediate complimentary courier pickup and send an expedited replacement or issue a 100% full refund including all original shipping charges.
